At Your Services Volunteer Scheduling

Self-service volunteer scheduling, day-of check-in, and the hours report your board and funders ask for.

Stop chasing volunteers and managing signups. Give your community a simple, self-service schedule they'll actually use — people claim and cancel their own spots, and the schedule keeps itself organized.

Overview

If you coordinate volunteers or staff, you know the drain: the back-and-forth email to fill a shift, the spreadsheet only one person can edit, too many people showing up for one task and nobody for another, and a printed roster that gets lost or re-typed. At Your Services Volunteer Scheduling takes that off your plate. It's a web-based schedule where people sign themselves in and out of activities on a shared calendar — from a phone if they like — while the app quietly prevents over-scheduling and shows each person exactly what their role should see. Volunteers manage only their own people, supervisors see the complete roster, and administrators keep control.

Features at a glance

  • Self-service scheduling — volunteers sign up and cancel from any phone, signing in with a password or an emailed link, and one account can cover a family or a whole crew.
  • Capacity limits & live status — per-activity maximums with live counts and color-coded status, so full shifts and gaps are obvious.
  • Privacy and delegation — volunteers see only their own sign-ups and private Jobs/Events stay hidden (enforced on the server); Supervisors each run their own.
  • Exceptions without touching the plan — lock a fully-staffed date, or cancel one, even an occupied one, with your reason emailed to everyone on it.
  • Change who is on a shift — take someone off before the day, sign up a volunteer who phones in, or add a walk-in on the day; either way the shift still goes ahead.
  • Bring guests — a volunteer signs up "+2" and the guests hold their places; staff take their names when they arrive, and add a guest with no account at all.
  • Change or end a program without losing people — an edit shows who it affects before it saves, the people affected can be emailed, and finished programs leave the calendar with their history kept.
  • Flexible recurrence & events — daily, weekday, day-of-week or monthly patterns, open-ended or fixed; dated multi-day events that can return next season.
  • Check-in and check-in sheets — record who actually showed up from a front-desk tablet, a volunteer's phone or the roster, or print the day's roster with write-in lines.
  • Hours, impact & fill-rate reports — monthly, quarterly and annual hours scheduled and checked in, estimated economic value, and fill percentages that show where to recruit.
  • Reminders that catch problems — an email about a day before each shift with an add-to-calendar file and a "Can't make it?" link; addresses the mail provider refuses, or later reports as bounced, are flagged and stopped.
  • Email you can see — every message waits its turn in a queue that survives restarts and keeps to your mail plan's daily and monthly allowance; an Email Activity page shows what was sent and how it went.
  • Bring your volunteer list with you — import Volunteer accounts from a spreadsheet saved as CSV, checked row by row before anything is created.
  • Accountability that keeps history — a tamper-evident admin audit trail, and deleting a Job/Event keeps its past so last quarter still reports correctly.
  • Secure, ad-free, and yours — hashed passwords, expiring sessions and server-side access control over HTTPS; no ads, ever; your data stays your organization's; your name, colors, logo and wording.

Security

Built with layered, industry-standard security. Passwords are never stored in readable form, sign-ins are protected against guessing and abuse, and every action is permission-checked on the server based on your role.

The security measures in detail
  • Passwords hashed (never stored in readable form); optional passwordless email sign-in with expiring links.
  • Signed-token sessions that expire automatically; protection against session theft (XSS); logout clears cached data.
  • Role-based access control enforced server-side; private Jobs/Events visible only to assigned users.
  • Rate limiting against brute-force and email-spam abuse; security HTTP headers; input validation and injection guards.
  • Information transmitted over HTTPS when deployed behind TLS.
