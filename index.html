How it works

Three steps, and no spreadsheet to keep straight.

  1. Post your shifts

    Recurring shifts, one-off events, or a monthly pattern — mapped once, in minutes.

  2. Volunteers sign up from their phone

    They pick the shift they want in a few taps — signing in with an emailed link, so there is no password to remember and no app to install.

  3. Watch the gaps close

    Every shift shows how full it is, so you see what's short weeks ahead instead of on the day.

Privacy & trust

Your organization's data belongs to your organization.

  • Volunteers only ever see their own shifts — they can see that a shift is 3 of 6 filled, but not who those three are, and never a program they are not part of. Their browser is not sent the other names at all, so it is not a matter of hiding them on the screen.
  • You can always see who changed what — a tamper-evident record of anyone who deleted a program or an account, changed someone's role, or called off a shift people had already signed up for.
  • Nobody is watching your volunteers — no ads, and nothing in the app reports their activity to anyone else.
