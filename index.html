About

At Your Services is the work of Russ McFadden — building focused, dependable software for organizations that coordinate people. The goal is simple: practical tools, sensible configuration, and security that's built in rather than bolted on. Small nonprofits rarely have dedicated IT staff, so setup and onboarding are currently provided free of charge — and free hosted options may be available for qualifying organizations. Just ask.
